Data Collection Sources & Dataset Provenance

The training data powering our neural network classification engine is collected and curated from established public NLP datasets and professional fact-checking organizations:

Kaggle Fake & Real News Dataset

Comprises over 44,000 news articles. Verified Real News is collected directly from official news wire services (such as Reuters), while Fake News articles are gathered from flagged misinformation portals and satirical sites across political, world news, and health domains.

PolitiFact & LIAR Dataset

Includes 12,800 human-annotated short statements and political headlines manually evaluated by professional journalists at PolitiFact.com, categorized across strict truthfulness metrics.

Cleaned NLP Corpus

Cleaned dataset spanning science, health, technology, finance, and global breaking news. Each article text undergoes automated lowercasing, HTML tag stripping, regex URL cleaning, and custom vocabulary tokenization.

Bidirectional LSTM Neural Architecture

Unlike simple keyword matching models, a Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (Bi-LSTM) neural network processes text in both forward and backward directions simultaneously. This allows the system to capture complex context, sensationalist phrasing, and syntactic patterns.

Raw Article Input ➔ Text Preprocessor ➔ Tokenizer Sequence (max_len=300)
  └──► Embedding Layer (vocab_size=5000, dim=128)
      └──► Bi-LSTM Layer 1 (64 hidden units x 2) + Dropout(0.3)
          └──► Bi-LSTM Layer 2 (32 hidden units x 2) + Dropout(0.3)
              └──► Global Average Pooling ➔ Dense(64, ReLU) ➔ Dense(1, Sigmoid)
                  └──► Probability Output: [0.0 = REAL | 1.0 = FAKE]

Responsible AI & Limitations Disclaimer

  • Confidence Ratings: The percentage shown reflects the probability assigned by the deep learning classifier based on learned textual patterns.
  • Domain Adaptation: Machine learning models trained on political and news domain datasets may exhibit lower precision when evaluating niche technical jargon or satirical comedy content.
  • Verification Guidance: This system is an AI assist tool and should always be paired with primary source verification and professional fact-checking organizations.
